## 1. How judging works (verified)

**Global Winners** are the top 10 projects, chosen by Executive Judges from the Global Finalists ([Space Apps 2025 Global Winners](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2025/awards/global-winners/)).

Award definitions relevant to BASELINE ([Judging & Awards Guide](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/judging-awards-guide), [Awards page](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2025/awards/)):

| Award | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Best Use of Science** | The project that makes the best and most valid use of science and/or the scientific method |
| Best Use of Data | Best makes space data accessible or leverages it for a unique application |
| Best Use of Technology | Most innovative use of technology |
| Best Mission Concept | Most plausible concept and design |
| Galactic Impact | Most potential to improve life on Earth or in the universe |

The guide also stresses that **presentation** is a judging criterion, alongside the other four.

## 2. 2025 Global Winners (verified list; descriptions from NASA)

Source: [NASA announcement](https://www.nasa.gov/learning-resources/stem-engagement-at-nasa/nasa-announces-2025-international-space-apps-challenge-global-winners/). Over 11,500 submissions; 10 winners.

| Award | Project | Country / event | What it did (NASA's description) | Relevance to BASELINE |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Best Use of Science | **SpaceGenes+** | Germany (Berlin) | Interactive dashboard showing how radiation and microgravity together affect astronauts at the molecular level | **High.** Astronaut health, science dashboard, won on *scientific validity* |
| Best Use of Data | Resonant Exoplanets | USA | AI pipeline over telescope/satellite data, including JWST spectra, to find exoplanets and flag possible biosignatures | Low; it rewards using real NASA data at scale |
| Best Use of Technology | Twisters (SkySense) | Mexico | NASA Earth-observation data + AI for hyperlocal forecasts, risk ratings, safest activity windows | Medium: "risk rating + what to do" decision support |
| Galactic Impact | Astro Sweepers | Universal Event | Public orbital data → debris compliance reports and a per-object risk index | Medium: explainable risk scoring |
| Best Mission Concept | PureFlow | Brazil | Systems-engineering platform to design and 3D-model habitats and test them against threats such as solar storms | Low |
| Most Inspirational | Photonics Odyssey | India (Chennai) | Satellite internet as national infrastructure for remote regions | Low (shows Indian teams can win) |
| Best Use of Storytelling | HerCode Space | Universal Event | Illustrated space-weather stories for children | Low |
| Global Connection | Gaia+LEO | USA | Optimisation of orbital and ground data-centre networks | Low |
| Art & Technology | Zumorroda-X | Egypt | Farming mini-games about climate adaptation | Low |
| Local Impact | QUEÑARIS | Peru | NASA satellite data + drones + AI for reforestation | Low |

**2024 example:** the Best Use of Science award went to WMPGang (Waterloo, Canada) for an orrery web app showing near-Earth objects ([NASA 2024 winners](https://www.nasa.gov/?p=802172)). Like SpaceGenes+, it is an interactive visual tool built on real science data.

## 3. What winning projects have in common (my interpretation, not verified)

1. **Real NASA or agency data is central**, not decoration. Every 2025 winner description names NASA or agency data, or a real-world system.
2. **One clear user and one clear question.** Each description fits in a single sentence.
3. **Science shown, not just cited.** SpaceGenes+ won *Best Use of Science* with a dashboard that *explains mechanisms*.
4. **A visual a judge remembers** (dashboard, 3D habitat, illustrated stories).

## 4. Lessons for BASELINE

| Lesson | Where BASELINE stands | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Real data matters (Best Use of Data / Science) | **Weak.** All astronaut data is simulated; the only "real" element is a published group mean | Say it plainly. Lead with *scientific method* (stress test), not data volume |
| "Most valid use of the scientific method" | Now **strong**: we test our own method against alternatives and show where it fails | Make the stress test a demo moment |
| One-sentence pitch | "Is this space, or is this me?" | Keep it |
| Memorable visual | Three-band chart + stress-test table | Show both; skip the body map |
| Presentation counts | A guided demo exists | Record a 2–3 minute video (see 07) |

**Realistic award fit:** *Best Use of Science* is the only plausible category, and only if the scientific-honesty story is told well.
